# Izuna Concept Board v0.1 - Review

**Asset:** `res://assets/characters/izuna/concept/izuna-concept-board-v01.png`  
**Status:** Awaiting director review  
**Generation mode:** Built-in OpenAI Image Generation  

## What Works

- Silhouette reads clearly at fighting-game scale.
- Human face remains visible and expressive.
- Shrine-warrior direction is distinct from a tokusatsu suit.
- Charcoal body mass, broken-white outer layer, and crimson action accents separate well.
- Asymmetrical garment creates motion without becoming a large cape.
- Clothing construction can be simplified into Skeleton2D body parts.
- Head ornament and partial porcelain motif suggest a fox without literal animal anatomy.
- Main pose communicates a fast, precise swordsman.

## Issues to Resolve

- The object in the left hand can read as a second sword; master design should clearly show one sword and one compact scabbard.
- Memory flame is attractive but still resembles ordinary fire. The next pass should use more fragmented calligraphy and broken-ring geometry.
- The three small explorations are costume variants, not true black silhouette studies.
- Main coat tail may be slightly long for clean hit-reaction animation and should be tested at gameplay scale.
- Small waist ornaments should be reduced before rigging to avoid animation noise.
- Mask ornament needs one original Covenant glyph locked across all future views.

## Director Decisions

Choose or comment on:

1. Overall face and hair direction.
2. Preferred small costume variant: left, center, or right.
3. Keep the half-mask on the head, move it to the waist, or reduce it further.
4. Keep the long white garment tail or shorten it for animation.

## Recommended Next Pass

Do not generate another broad concept board. After director feedback, create one clean master design that:

- Uses one sword and an unmistakable scabbard.
- Combines the selected costume route with the approved face.
- Simplifies waist accessories.
- Replaces fire-like wisps with memory-calligraphy fragments.
- Keeps hands, feet, weapon, and garment layers fully readable.
