# Izuna Master Concept v0.1 - Review

**Asset:** `res://assets/characters/izuna/concept/izuna-master-concept-v01.png`  
**Status:** Approved and locked by director on June 16, 2026  
**References:** Concept board v0.1 and director's first prototype

## Successful Synthesis

- Style, human face, anatomy, and rendering remain consistent with concept board v0.1.
- Fox-deity identity from the director prototype is immediately visible.
- Porcelain mask reads clearly without becoming a permanent helmet.
- One katana and one scabbard are now unambiguous.
- Short crimson shoulder mantle connects the old and new costume languages.
- White garment is shorter and more animation-friendly.
- Three memory-tail manifestations preserve a clear body silhouette.
- Character remains original and no longer reads as a Kamen Rider suit.

## Minor Production Notes

- Neutral stance is slightly upright; idle key pose should lower the center of gravity.
- Tail wisps should become more fragmented and calligraphic in the VFX design pass.
- Waist ornaments can be simplified further when body parts are separated for rigging.
- The long sword tip requires generous frame padding during animation exports.
- The shoulder mantle and white sleeve must remain separate rig layers.

## Canonical Decision

This image is the **canonical visual master for Izuna**. Do not regenerate or redesign the character broadly. Apply remaining production adjustments only during turnaround, rig separation, VFX development, and key-pose production.

All future Izuna assets must preserve:

- Face, hair, and crimson streak.
- Side-mounted porcelain fox mask.
- Charcoal base costume and broken-white asymmetrical layer.
- Compact crimson shoulder mantle.
- Katana, scabbard, and broken-ring hardware language.
- Three neutral memory-tail wisps and nine-tail Ultimate escalation.
